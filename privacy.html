Account Privacy

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 17 August 2026

This notice explains how personal data is used when you create and use a WHR Army Builder account. WHR Army Builder is a free, unofficial fan project.

What we collect

  • Your email address, used for account authentication and account-related messages.
  • A display name that you choose for use within account and sharing features.
  • Armies and related roster data that you choose to save to your account.
  • Technical account identifiers and timestamps needed to operate the service, including when the account was last actively used.

We do not need your real name, postal address, date of birth or other unnecessary personal information to provide an account.

Why we use it

We use this information to create and secure your account, provide cloud army storage, let you manage your saved armies, and provide sharing and campaign features that you choose to use. The intended lawful basis for core account processing is performance of the service you request by creating and using an account. Where a different lawful basis is required for a future feature, this notice will be updated before that feature is introduced.

Private and shared armies

New cloud-saved armies are private by default. You can explicitly mark an army as shared. Your email address is not intended to be shown to other users; sharing features use your chosen display name instead.

Who processes the data

Account authentication and cloud data are hosted using Supabase. This means account information and saved-army data are processed using Supabase infrastructure on behalf of WHR Army Builder. Other infrastructure providers may process ordinary web-server or delivery data required to serve the site.

How long we keep it

Account data and cloud-saved armies are kept while your account remains active or until you delete the relevant content. To avoid retaining dormant account data indefinitely, an account that has not been actively used for 18 months is treated as inactive.

When an account reaches that inactivity point, we will normally send a warning to the registered email address and schedule the account for deletion 30 days later. Signing in during that 30-day period cancels the scheduled deletion and resets the inactivity period.

If there is no sign-in during the warning period, the account and associated WHR Army Builder cloud data are automatically deleted. Limited technical backup, logging or security retention may continue for a short period where this is required by infrastructure providers or for security purposes.

Your choices and rights

You can delete individual saved armies and change an army between Private and Shared. You can also request deletion of your account. UK data-protection law may give you rights including access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability where applicable.

Security and data minimisation

WHR Army Builder aims to collect only the information needed for account functionality. Database access controls are used so that users cannot edit another user's private account data. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not stored in WHR Army Builder's application tables.

Contact and complaints

During development, privacy enquiries can be raised through the WHR Army Builder project owner/community contact. A dedicated privacy contact address will be published before public account registration is treated as a production service. If you are in the UK, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office about the handling of your personal data.

Development notice: Account functionality is currently being developed and tested. This privacy notice will be reviewed again before the account system is promoted from the development site to the public production site.
