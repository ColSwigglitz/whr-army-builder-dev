Free Forever

WHR Army Builder will always be free to use

This is a fan project made for the Warhammer Renaissance community, not a commercial product. The Army Builder is free today and the intention is for it to remain free in perpetuity: no subscription, no paid tier and no paywall will ever be required to build, save or print an army.

If you enjoy using WHR Army Builder and would like to help support its continued development, hosting and maintenance, you can buy me a coffee on Ko-fi. Support is entirely optional and will never affect access to the application or its features.
